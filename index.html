--- layout: default title: MiniPad — Native macOS Launchpad --- # 🚀 MiniPad **Native macOS Launchpad** — a modern, beautiful app launcher for macOS 13+. [Download MiniPad](#download) ## ✨ Features - 🎨 **Launchpad Grid** — drag-and-drop reordering with smooth animations - ⭐ **Favorites** — star your most-used apps, pinned to the top in order - 👆 **Multi-Select Shake Mode** — long-press to shake, tap to select multiple, drag all together - 📁 **Folders** — create, rename, delete; drag apps in/out - 🔍 **Search & Categories** — find any app instantly - 🎨 **Custom Icons** — assign your own icon to any app - 🌓 **Themes** — Dark, Light, and Liquid Glass - 🕐 **Digital Clock** — centered top bar with optional seconds - ⚙️ **Settings** — launch at login, grid density, sort modes ## 📦 Installation 1. Download the [latest release](https://github.com/egulsen10k-lgtm/MiniPad/releases/latest) (`MiniPad-Installer.dmg`) 2. Open the DMG and drag **MiniPad** to **Applications** 3. Launch **MiniPad** from Applications ## 🔧 Requirements - macOS 13.0+ - Apple Silicon or Intel Mac ## 🔗 Links - [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/egulsen10k-lgtm/MiniPad) - [Releases](https://github.com/egulsen10k-lgtm/MiniPad/releases) - [Issues](https://github.com/egulsen10k-lgtm/MiniPad/issues) --- *Built with ❤️ for macOS*
